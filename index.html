Outcomes

  • LO1. Analyse real-world business challenges; develop evidence-based, innovative solutions.
  • LO2. Apply advanced techniques and interdisciplinary knowledge to contemporary business problems.

Team project (50% — the spine)

  • Teams of 4–5: AI helpdesk, vision QC, ERP rollout, hospital MIS, or campus app.
  • Artifacts: charter → WBS → Gantt → cost sheet → comms/RACI → risk + SLA → final deck (Sessions 19–20).
  • Graded 10 (problem grasp) + 10 (tool use) + 30 (solution).

Cases

  • C1 Waterfall vs Agile · C2 When Agile harms learning · C3 Options thinking — HBR cases depending on availability, else other cases will be discussed.
  • C4 Pharma-plant composite (governance/vendors) · C5 AI-pilot composite — classroom-safe, no permission needed.

Sessions (20 × 90 min) — what each session covers

Click any session to see what will be covered plus the reading / case.

01Introduction to IT Project Management

What will be covered: What is a project, programme and portfolio; the triple constraint (scope-cost-time); why large digital projects fail; team formation and project idea pitch.

Reading / case: Schwalbe Ch.1.

02Project Management & IT Context I

What will be covered: Systems approach; product vs project life cycles; Waterfall in depth; teams draft the life cycle for their own project.

Reading / case: Schwalbe Ch.2 (part).

03Project Management & IT Context II

What will be covered: Agile, Scrum and Hybrid methods; structured debate on which method fits which context.

Reading / case: Schwalbe Ch.2 + Case C1.

04Process Groups & Knowledge Areas I

What will be covered: The five process groups (Initiating to Closing) mapped to knowledge areas; drafting a project charter and RACI.

Reading / case: Schwalbe Ch.3 (part).

05Process Groups & Methodology II

What will be covered: Tailoring a methodology to project size and risk; what goes wrong when Agile is scaled carelessly.

Reading / case: Schwalbe Ch.3 + Case C2.

06Integration Management & Selection

What will be covered: Strategic alignment; NPV, IRR and payback for project selection; thinking in options under uncertainty; teams score three options.

Reading / case: Schwalbe Ch.4 + Case C3.

07Scope Management

What will be covered: Eliciting requirements; defining scope; building the Work Breakdown Structure; teams submit WBS v1.

Reading / case: Schwalbe Ch.5.

08Schedule Management I

What will be covered: Defining and sequencing activities; network diagrams; finding the critical path (hands-on exercise).

Reading / case: Schwalbe Ch.6 (part).

09Schedule Management II

What will be covered: Gantt charts and milestones; crashing vs fast-tracking; teams build the Gantt for their project.

Reading / case: Schwalbe Ch.6 (part).

10Cost Management

What will be covered: Analogous, parametric and bottom-up estimating; budgeting and reserves.

Reading / case: Schwalbe Ch.7.

11Quality Management

What will be covered: Plan, manage and control quality; QA vs QC; writing a test plan for an AI-project scenario.

Reading / case: Schwalbe Ch.8.

12IT Governance & Compliance

What will be covered: COBIT and ITIL overview; GDPR and HIPAA essentials; regulated-industry case discussion.

Reading / case: Instructor handouts + C4.

13Cybersecurity Management

What will be covered: Common threats and vulnerabilities; security policies; incident response and recovery tabletop.

Reading / case: Instructor handouts.

14Real-World Applications I

What will be covered: Walkthrough of computer-vision and AI projects; the scope-schedule-cost-quality trade-off in practice.

Reading / case: All Ch.1-8 + C5.

15Resource Management

What will be covered: Resource planning and estimation; team charters; motivation, conflict and leading people.

Reading / case: Schwalbe Ch.9.

16Communications & Leadership

What will be covered: Communication planning; stakeholder matrix; leadership skills for IT managers; teams draft their comms plan.

Reading / case: Schwalbe Ch.10.

17ITSM, Change & Failure

What will be covered: IT service management and SLAs; why IT projects fail; the change-management approach and success factors.

Reading / case: Handouts + C4.

18Outsourcing, Vendors & Finance

What will be covered: Outsourcing strategies; vendor selection and contracts; budgeting, ROI and TCO analysis.

Reading / case: Handouts.

19Final Project Presentations I

What will be covered: First half of teams present their proposal (problem, WBS, schedule, cost, risk, governance); peer and instructor feedback.

Reading / case: All chapters.

20Final Presentations II + Wrap

What will be covered: Remaining teams present; course wrap-up and end-term briefing.

Reading / case: All chapters.

Agentic AI as Teaching Assistant

An Agentic AI Teaching Assistant supports this course alongside the instructor — not as a replacement for teaching, but as an always-available helper, grounded on the course material (Schwalbe chapters, session readings, case briefs, project milestones):

  • Doubt-clearing after every session — guided explanations with pointers back to the reading, not shortcut answers (critical path, NPV vs options thinking, RACI, SLA drafting…).
  • Team-project coach — feedback on drafts at each milestone (charter, WBS, Gantt, cost sheet, comms plan, risk register, SLA sketch) before instructor review.
  • Case prep partner — helps teams prepare the methodology debate and discussion questions for C1–C3, and walks through the C4–C5 composites.
  • Assessment literacy — explains rubrics and what "exceeds expectation" looks like, without writing student work.

Ground rules: the AI TA never grades and never writes final submissions; judgment calls and evaluation stay with the instructor. Integrity rules (max 20% referenced content) apply to AI-assisted work too — the TA coaches the process, students own the output.
